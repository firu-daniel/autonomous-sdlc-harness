**Problem.** None of the six files is in the tree. `.gitignore` excludes the whole directory (`git check-ignore -v harness-runs/scratch/task19_figures.mjs` prints `.gitignore:65:harness-runs/scratch/*`), and `git ls-files harness-runs/scratch` lists only `harness-runs/scratch/README.md`. `scripts/scratch-run.sh`'s own header says why: *"`<state_dir>/scratch/` is gitignored by its contents, so nothing run from here reaches a commit"*. The files exist only in the worktree that ran this branch. On `dev`, on `main` and in any other checkout, each of the six commands fails with a missing-file error.

These are not side details. They are the stated provenance of the branch's three headline records:
- the verdict arithmetic (site f);
- the calibration's `cannot-separate` result and its 101-point table (site a);
- every arm A spread, give-up, tool-call and cost figure (site e).

`.claude/context/conventions.md` → `## Documents of record` requires *"A measured fact states what was measured, the command and the exact message"*. Here the command names a file no reader can have.

**Why the committed review did not catch it.** The code review's Pass 0 accepted `summarizeVariant`, `calibrateThreshold`, `readPerQuery`, `partitionOf` and `breakdown` having no committed caller on the grounds that *"Each is called by the run's documented scratch launchers, as the eval's own launcher route prescribes."* The launcher *route* is documented. These launchers are not: their source appears nowhere in the tree. So `evals/docs-retrieval/calibrate.mjs` and `evals/docs-retrieval/arm-a/spread.mjs` have no committed route that reaches them, and the figures they produced cannot be regenerated from the published tree.

**The precedent this departs from.** Every other scratch launcher the eval's documents cite has its source published beside the command:
- On `dev`, `docs/retrieval-eval.md` gives `harness-runs/scratch/cold-build.mjs` ("Create `harness-runs/scratch/cold-build.mjs`:") and `harness-runs/scratch/query-log-pass.mjs` in fenced blocks. `docs/retrieval-eval-results.md` then cites both commands.
- This branch does the same for `harness-runs/scratch/comparability.mjs`, in `docs/retrieval-eval.md` → `## Running arm A by hand` ("The launcher, `harness-runs/scratch/comparability.mjs`:", followed by a fenced `js` block).

The six launchers above are the only cited ones without that treatment.

**Who reaches the wrong answer.** Two readers:
- A maintainer re-checking the *withdrawn* verdict before executing roadmap item 18 (`docs/development.md` → `## 6. The roadmap this tree defers to`) runs the published command and gets a missing file. They cannot tell whether a figure was computed as described or typed by hand.
- The branch that executes item 18 removes `ABSTAIN_SCORE_THRESHOLD`. It needs the calibration record to be reproducible first, and it is not.
