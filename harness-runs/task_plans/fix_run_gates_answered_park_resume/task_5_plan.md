**Goal:** Guard the new route with a test. It asserts that the wire strings Tasks 1–4 introduced are spelled identically in the files that produce them and the files that consume them. **Depends on:** Tasks 1, 2, 3 and 4. Their strings are the contract this suite pins, restated:

- **The clause.** `Park answer: <question_file> + <answer_file>.` is appended to both prompt forms in `plugin/agents/test-fix-plan-writer.md` → `## Invocation contract` (Task 1) **and** in `plugin/instructions/plan_orchestration_instructions_core.md` → `### G.2 Write and review the test fix plan` (Task 2). Park answer: <question_file> + <answer_file>.` and `Revise the test fix plan at <test_fix_plan_path> per architecture findings: <findings_file>. Park answer: <question_file> + <answer_file>.` The agent spells the log as `<log_path>` and the earlier logs as `<comma-separated earlier-round log paths, or none>`, so the pin compares the clause, not the whole line.
- **The marker.** `Park answer consumed:` appears in the agent's process step (Task 1) and in G.0's pending-answer test, `grep -c '^Park answer consumed:'` (Task 2).
- **The origin.** `Phase G test-fix-plan-writer` appears in G.2's escalation wording (Task 2), in both autonomous implementation forks (Tasks 3 and 4), and in the task-flow planning fork's `Override 2(a)` exclusion (Task 3). The two other Phase G origins, `Phase G gates` (G.1) and `Phase G architecture-reviewer gate` (G.2's gate cap), are named by Task 2 and quoted by Tasks 3 and 4; this suite does not pin them.

- **The cap paragraph.** `### G.5 Re-run, and the round cap` keeps its bold lead sentence `**An answered park never buys a fresh cap.**`, and the paragraph it opens now covers the answered `fixable: 0` park (Task 2).
