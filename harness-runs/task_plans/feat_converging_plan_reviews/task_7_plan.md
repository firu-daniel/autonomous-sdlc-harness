### Task 7 — Carry the revision notes in the remote bundle's planning paths, and keep a job restore from mixing a committed round with a newer draft, and log what it withholds

**Goal:** Keep the remote state bundle's planning paths a mirror of the planning loop's staging lists — which now include `<state_dir>/plan_revisions/<branch>/` — and, now that the task-plan files are committed at every round's end and on a planning pause, make a job restore leave the task-plan files on disk from **one** revision: once the checkout tracks the story index, the committed round is the whole task-plan state, and the bundle's newer drafts never land beside it.

**Why the restore changes.** Before this branch every task-plan file was untracked until convergence, so a restore always brought back one consistent revision. Now a job can end between a writer's revision and the next round commit without taking a planning-pause commit — a job killed mid-round, whose post-step still uploads the bundle (`docs/remote-execution.md` → **Central state.**). Under today's rule (*a draft is placed only where nothing exists*), the next job would keep the committed story index and modified per-task files but place the revision's new per-task files and its revision note, and continue the saved walk over that mixture. Task 18 and Task 19 make an overload self-pause commit the round so far; this task covers what no session-side commit can — the killed job — at the restore, so the recovery holds in a remote job as in a local run (`harness-runs/lessons.md` → *"A recovery for an unattended run's failure covers every execution mode the failure reaches"*).

**Where this task stops.** The fork prose that adds `plan_revisions/<branch>/` to the per-round, pause and convergence staging lists is **Task 19**'s; the planning-pause commit on an overload self-pause is **Task 18**'s (§2.5) and **Task 19**'s (the fork's overload paragraph); the decision record in `docs/remote-execution.md` is **Task 29**'s; this checkout's self-adopted `scripts/lib/harness-run-lib.sh` and `scripts/remote-run.sh` are **Task 27**'s. **The readers that grade or specify the restore's output are Task 32's**, not this task's: `scripts/lib/gate12-sandbox-round/grade-full-run.sh` → `g12_fr6_restore` and its comment (which grade a restore with no `placed <n> planning file(s)` line as FR6 `fail` when the parked bundle holds a planning file — exactly what this task's tracked arm prints), the FR6 row of `docs/sandbox-verification.md`, and the two FR6 specification bullets of `docs/development.md`; the `cli/test/gate12-sandbox-round-full-run.test.mjs` case that pins a withheld restore as an FR6 pass is **Task 33**'s.
