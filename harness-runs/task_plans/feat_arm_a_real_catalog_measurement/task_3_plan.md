- [ ] Same sub-heading — the statistics the rule does not name, fixed here: relevance's margins are **`1 / positives` of recall@5** and **`0.5 / positives` of MRR** (one query moving from rank 1 to rank 2), `positives` being the pooled positive count of the set used, and the lead must **exceed** each; a variant's **per-query wall time** is, per repetition, the nearest-rank p50 of that repetition's per-query `durationMs`, and the cost ratio is arm E's p95 over the stronger variant's median of those; a variant's **token cost per query** is, per repetition, the sum over records of `input_tokens + output_tokens + cache_creation_input_tokens + cache_read_input_tokens` divided by the record count (every usage field is also reported separately), the variant's figure the median; **failure** compares arm E's `abstainedOnNegative / negatives` against the stronger variant's median share of negatives answered `none`, and arm E must be at least as high. Mark this block as the reading that was chosen, with the date.
- [ ] `### The re-calibration method, fixed before the real-catalog run` — the task prompt's item 5, with its readings fixed: the score is `bestRerankScore` (the top reranker score `fused-rerank` compares, recorded whether or not it abstained); a positive's **best score** is its `bestRerankScore`; a point with `bestRerankScore: null` (no candidate to rerank) is excluded and listed by id. **Separable** — every negative's score below every positive's → the new value is the midpoint of the highest negative and the lowest positive, rounded half-up to two decimals. **Overlapping** — `L` is the lowest best score among positives carrying a grade-3 label; `N` is the `near` negatives scoring below `L`; the value is the smallest two-decimal number strictly greater than every score in `N`; the **price** is every positive (any grade) whose score falls below that value, counted over the pooled set and published by id; if `N` is empty, or the price is **more than one positive query**, the finding is that *a threshold cannot separate this catalog* and the constant is left unchanged. The pool is the real catalog **plus** the two committed corpora, each regenerated with the uncensored field first, and the committed sets' negatives are classed `far` / `near` by meaning **before** their scores become observable. On a *withdrawn* verdict the distributions are recorded and the constant goes with the tool.
