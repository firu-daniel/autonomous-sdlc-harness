### Task 4 — Strip and scrub the session's environment in `spawn_engine`, and act on a push-refusal record

**Goal:** In `cli/templates/scripts/autonomous-watcher.sh`, three changes:

**Where the sets are read.** Every read and every refusal happens in `spawn_engine`'s **own** shell, never inside the launch subshell. The `{ … }` group that `exec`s `"$AGENT_CLI"` is the head of the `{ … } | tee … | "$formatter"` pipeline, inside the `( … ) &` subshell. - **What.** `spawn_engine` computes one `local` indexed array, `session_unset_names`, of the names the group will unset:

- **Refusals.** Either reader failing does what the existing pre-launch refusals do: `spawn_refuse "$branch" "<reason>"` then `return 1`.
