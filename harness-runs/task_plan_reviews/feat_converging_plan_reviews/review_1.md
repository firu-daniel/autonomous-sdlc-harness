Review 0 raised the cap-park half as Should Fix 5, and it went unaddressed.

Give each a reason. Either keep `R+1` and say that the cap counts rounds over the branch's whole planning history, or give the writer entry a cap base the way Task 12's `cap_base` does for the fix-plan loop, and name where that base is held.    The prompt says: *"Its restore already never writes over a committed file. *Verify* that this still holds once the task-plan files are committed, and state what the bundle then carries."* Task 7 and Task 29 verify the mechanism (*"a draft is placed only where nothing exists"*) and call the bundle's copy *"a fallback a job restore keeps behind the committed file"*. The restore keeps the committed (older) story index and modified per-task files, but places the revision's new untracked per-task files and its `task_plan_revision_<n+1>.md`.
