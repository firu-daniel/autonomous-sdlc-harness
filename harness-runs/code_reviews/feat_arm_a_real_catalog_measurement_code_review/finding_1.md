### 1. "Every relevance column" is false: arm E beats arm D on two strict columns, one of them on the real catalog

**File:** `docs/retrieval-eval-results.md` → `## The shipped default against fusion alone`: the `**What this section records.**` paragraph ("scores **below** arm D, `fused`, on every relevance column") and the `**What the real catalog did to the trade: narrowed it from both sides, and reversed neither.**` paragraph ("D still\nleads E on every relevance column").
**Also:** `docs/retrieval.md` → `## Measured, and how`, the measured-facts bullet opening "The shipped default mode `fused-rerank` measured against `fused`" ("on all three it scores lower on every relevance column"). And `docs/retrieval-eval.md` → `## The decision rule` → `### Two arm A variants, and how they combine`, the paragraph opening "**The rule compares E to A; the recorded figures also bear on which mode is the default.**" ("outscores the shipped `fused-rerank`\ndefault on every relevance column").

**Problem.** `docs/retrieval-eval.md` → `## What each metric means` defines the relevance columns: the four primary ones (recall@1, recall@3, recall@5, MRR, which score `grade >= 1`) and the two `strict` columns (strict recall@5 and strict MRR, which score `grade == 3`). Every generated arm table carries all six. The generated region of `docs/retrieval-eval-results.md` shows arm E ahead of arm D on two strict columns:

| Corpus | Column | D `fused` | E `fused-rerank` |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gate10-catalog` | strict recall@5 | 0.659 | **0.682** |
| `self-docs` | strict MRR | 0.363 | **0.383** |

(`### Corpus \`gate10-catalog\`` table, rows `D` and `E`; `### Corpus \`self-docs\`` table, rows `D` and `E`.) On every primary column, and on the other strict cells, E is below D on all three corpora.

This branch widened the claim to cover the real catalog (the results section's opening paragraph and its new "What the real catalog did" paragraph, and the `docs/retrieval.md` bullet rewritten to "on all three"). That makes the claim false on the one corpus the branch added. The false sentence is the section's headline measured fact, and the section says the open default-mode decision "would be decided, against this table". A maintainer taking that decision reads that fusion dominates the reranker on every relevance measure. That is wrong: on the real catalog the reranker puts a grade-3 (answering) section in the top five more often than fusion does. The row pair in the section omits the strict columns, so a reader cannot see the exception in the section itself.

**Fix.** Qualify each claim to the primary columns and record the two strict exceptions once, in the results section. Change no generated byte: every edit is outside `<!-- eval:generated:start -->` / `<!-- eval:generated:end -->`.
